Gallagher LLP

Overview

What Is Rocky?

Rocky is a virtual paralegal that runs on a dedicated office computer. Every afternoon, she reads the day's case-related emails, organizes documents into the right folders, and writes a summary of the day's activity — so the attorney can review everything in one place before end of day.

💻

Runs Unattended

Rocky operates on her own dedicated laptop in the office — always on, always connected. No one needs to open the program or click "run." She works on a set daily schedule, automatically.

📂

Organizes, Never Decides

Rocky reads, sorts, and summarizes. She does not make legal decisions, send communications to clients, or take action on any case. The attorney reviews everything Rocky produces and decides what happens next.

Email Access

How Rocky Reads Email

Rocky has her own Microsoft 365 account (rocky@gallagherllp.com) with read-only access to the attorney's inbox. She connects through Microsoft's standard secure cloud system — the same one Outlook and Teams use.

Outlook rules (set up by the attorney) automatically sort incoming mail into per-case folders. Rocky reads from those folders — she never touches the main inbox.

Technology

How Does Rocky "Think"?

Rocky uses Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, to read and understand documents. This is the same type of technology that powers many professional tools in use across industries today.

🧠

Rocky Uses AI To:

  • Read and understand emails and attachments
  • Decide where each document belongs in the case file
  • Write plain-English summaries of case activity
  • Flag items that need the attorney's attention
👤

What Rocky Does Not Do

Rocky does not make legal decisions, give legal advice, or take any action on a case. She organizes and summarizes. The attorney reviews everything and decides what happens next.

📝

Guided by Written Rules

Rocky's behavior is controlled by plain-English instruction files the attorney writes and can update at any time. Changes take effect within minutes — no technical work required.

Safety & Guardrails

What Rocky Cannot Do — By Design

Rocky's limitations are enforced at the system level, not just in her programming. Even if the software had a bug, these actions are blocked by Microsoft 365 itself.

🚫

Cannot Send Email as the Attorney

The permission to send from the attorney's account does not exist in the system and never will. This is not a setting that can be accidentally turned on.

🚫

Cannot Email Anyone Outside the Firm

When Rocky eventually gains the ability to send from her own account, a Microsoft mail-flow rule blocks all messages to non-firm addresses.

🚫

Cannot Modify or Delete Emails

Rocky has read-only access to the attorney's mailbox. She cannot move, edit, or delete any email in the inbox or its folders.

🚫

Cannot Take Legal Action

Rocky organizes documents and writes summaries. She does not file anything with a court, respond to opposing counsel, or communicate with clients.

✅

Emergency Shut-Off

Sending an email with the subject "ROCKY STOP" immediately shuts Rocky down. She stays off until manually restarted or a "ROCKY START" email is sent.

✅

Full Audit Trail

Every action Rocky takes is logged — every email she reads, every file she moves, every summary she writes. The logs are always available for review.
